Good to know

FAQs

Quick answers to the questions guests ask most.

Booking

How do I book a room, a table or an event?

Use the booking page, call us on {{phoneDisplay}} or message us on WhatsApp. Online bookings are requests: our team checks availability and contacts you to confirm.

Is my booking confirmed as soon as I send it?

Not yet. You receive a reference straight away, and the booking is confirmed once our team has contacted you. You can follow its status under My booking.

How much do rooms cost?

Rates depend on the room type and your dates. Send a booking request or call us and we will give you the price before anything is confirmed.

How do I change or cancel a booking?

Open My booking, enter your reference and phone number, and cancel from there. To change dates or details, call or WhatsApp us with your reference.

Your stay

What is included with a room?

Every room has free Wi-Fi, a smart TV with digital channels, a private bathroom with a shower, complimentary toiletries, bottled water and daily housekeeping. Breakfast and parking are included.

Can I arrive late at night?

Yes. The front desk is open 24 hours, so you can check in whenever you get off the highway. Let us know roughly when to expect you.

Is there parking?

Yes, parking on site is free for guests.

Is there a swimming pool?

Yes, we have an outdoor pool beside the garden.

Is smoking allowed?

The hotel is smoke-free indoors.

Food and drink

Can I eat at the restaurant if I am not staying?

Of course. Travellers and local guests are welcome in the restaurant, the garden and the bar. You can reserve a table or simply walk in.

Can I order food online?

Yes. Choose from the menu and tell us whether you are eating in, collecting, or want it delivered to your room. You pay at the inn.

Events

How many guests can you host?

Up to 200 guests, using the garden, the poolside and our indoor spaces. We also have a conference room for meetings and trainings.

When are the live music nights?

We host a live Kamba and Benga concert every month. Dates and artists are announced on our Instagram and Facebook pages.

Finding us

Where exactly are you?

At Mbuinzau on Mombasa Road in Kibwezi, Makueni County, about 100 metres from the highway beneath Mbuinzau Hill. Open directions in Google Maps.

Still have a question?

Our front desk is open 24 hours.
