Privacy policy

What we collect when you use this website, and what we do with it.

Who we are

{{name}}, {{address}}, is responsible for the personal information collected through this website.

What we collect

  • Bookings: your name, phone number, email address if you give one, the details of your booking (dates, room type, number of guests, event details) and any requests you add.
  • Food orders: your name, phone number, what you ordered, your table or room number if given, and any notes.
  • Reviews: the name you enter, your rating and what you write.

This website does not take card or mobile-money payments, so we do not collect payment details here. It does not use advertising or tracking cookies. The only cookie is one that keeps our own staff signed in to the staff dashboard.

How we use it

  • To check availability, confirm your booking or order, and contact you about it.
  • To provide the service you asked for during your visit.
  • To publish your review, once approved, with the name you gave.
  • To keep the records a business is required to keep.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it to send marketing messages.

Who can see it

Your booking and order details are visible only to Viewpoint Inn staff who need them to serve you. We share information outside the inn only where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep booking and order records for as long as they are needed for the purposes above and to meet our legal and accounting obligations, and then delete them.

Other websites

This website links to WhatsApp, Google Maps, social media and travel booking sites. When you follow those links, the other company's own privacy policy applies. The map on our contact page is provided by Google.

Your rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you may ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. To do so, call us on {{phoneDisplay}} or speak to the front desk, and quote your booking reference if you have one.
