Welcome

The best joint along Mombasa Road

Viewpoint Inn sits at Mbuinzau, a short turn off the Nairobi–Mombasa highway in Kibwezi. Whether you are breaking a long drive, in town for work or here for the weekend, you get a comfortable room, a hot meal and a quiet night.

Step outside and the view does the rest: green lawns, the pool, and Mbuinzau Hill rising behind the inn.
